Projects

Active

vcalib: calibration filters for RF-DETR under illumination shift

A ~2 KB differentiable filter that undoes an illumination shift before the image reaches a frozen detector. No retraining, no labels, minutes on a CPU.

  • 35.9% activation recovery on real held-out image pairs
  • Closes the AP gap on level_2 (SAM3 pseudo-GT)
  • ~3.5 min to fit on a laptop CPU

Misinformation detection in fiction with LLM-generated ontologies

An LLM + RAG architecture that builds a verified ontology incrementally and uses it to flag false claims in films, podcasts and texts. Published at DCAI'25.

  • Precision 0.98 · F1 0.86 on 320 evaluation sentences
  • 246-class ontology generated without supervision
